# 📐 Calculus I Notes — Weeks 1 to 10

Personal lecture notes for a 10-week Calculus I course. Written in LaTeX, compiled to PDF, and organized by week. Made for myself, shared in case they help someone else.

## 👤 Author

**Bakdaulet Sotsial** — student at Astana IT University.

## 📚 Topics

| Week | Topic |
|:----:|:------|
| 1 | Functions: sets, functions and their graphs, shifts and scaling graphs, elementary and transcendental functions, inverse functions, composition of functions, trigonometric functions[cite: 2] |
| 2 | Sequences and Limits: sequences converging to a limit, properties of convergent sequences, monotone sequences, limits of function values, definition of a limit, the sandwich theorem[cite: 2] |
| 3 | Limits and Continuity: continuous functions, one-sided limits, limits involving infinity, relative rates of growth (table of equivalent functions)[cite: 2] |
| 4 | Derivatives: definition of derivative, differentiation rules, derivatives of trigonometric and transcendental functions, chain rule, higher order derivatives, implicit differentiation, linearization and differentials, parametric functions, tangents and normal lines[cite: 2] |
| 5 | Applications of Derivatives: maxima and minima, mean value theorem, curve sketching using first and second derivatives, asymptotes and dominant terms, rational functions, related rates, optimization, indeterminate forms and L'Hôpital's rule[cite: 2] |
| 6 | Indefinite Integrals: antiderivatives, indefinite integrals, basic integration formulas, substitution method[cite: 2] |
| 7 | Techniques of Integration: integration by parts, special cases, integrals of trigonometric and rational functions, partial fractions[cite: 2] |
| 8 | Definite Integrals: area and finite sums, sigma notation, Riemann sums, fundamental theorem of calculus, substitution in definite integrals, improper integrals[cite: 2] |
| 9 | Applications of Integrals: area between curves, volumes of solids of revolution, arc length, area of surfaces of revolution[cite: 2] |
| 10 | Complex Numbers & Polar Coordinates: complex numbers and operations, polar coordinate equations and graphing, integration in polar coordinates, area and lengths in polar coordinates[cite: 2] |
